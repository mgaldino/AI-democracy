```
**A3 (Information structure).** Following Morris and Shin (2003, Section 2.1), the prior on $\omega_t$ is improper uniform. The signal family satisfies monotone likelihood ratio, and higher $\sigma$ Blackwell-degrades private signals.^[The mechanism is informational, not preferential: $E$ desires revolt equally under both shock types ($u_E(P)$ is the same). What differs is the capacity to coordinate, determined by the information structure of the shock. The improper uniform prior yields uniqueness for all $\sigma > 0$ and permits closed-form comparative statics via the Laplacian property (Morris and Shin 2003, Proposition 2.1).]

Under A3, conditional on others using a cutoff strategy, the visibility event $\{\pi_t \geq \bar{\pi}\}$ is increasing in the fundamental $\omega$ (by MLRP). Combined with the dominance regions established in Section 2.2, these conditions satisfy Morris and Shin (2003, Theorem 2.2), yielding Lemma 1 (Section 3).
```

```
**A4 (Majority-cost condition).** $\gamma \geq c_s\bar{\varphi}$. The non-exposed majority compensates when the threat from $E$ is credible ($\ell_t = L$ and $g(\pi_t) = 1$). The full voting derivation appears in Appendix A.

**A5 (Risk dominance).** For each regime $x$, the visibility threshold satisfies $\bar{\pi} < b_x/(b_x + m)$: the coalition club good strictly exceeds the failed-mobilization cost at the visibility margin.^[This ensures that risk dominance selects participation in the low-noise limit (Morris and Shin 2003). When $\bar{\pi} \geq b_x/(b_x + m)$, coordination fails even under common knowledge of the shock.]

**A6 (Trajectory separation).** Signal noise satisfies $\sigma_r < \min\{\hat{\sigma}_D, \hat{\sigma}_A\}$ and $\sigma_\tau > \max\{\hat{\sigma}_D, \hat{\sigma}_A\}$, where $\hat{\sigma}_x$ are the endogenous noise thresholds derived in Lemma 2 (Section 3). Rapid displacement is predictable: the approaching automation is visible and workers organize as the shock approaches ($\sigma_r$ low). Threshold automation is genuinely surprising: the complementary phase masks the coming substitution until the threshold is crossed ($\sigma_\tau$ high). This is the single reduced-form informational bridge from the economic premise to the political model.
```
